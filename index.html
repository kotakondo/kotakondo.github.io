Kota Kondo

Stanford Multi-Robot Systems Lab | VLMs | VLAs | World Models | Manipulation | Humanoids

I am a postdoc in the Multi-Robot Systems Lab (MSL) at Stanford University, working with Professor Mac Schwager. My research focuses on vision-language models (VLMs), vision-language-action models (VLAs), and world models for robot manipulation and humanoid robots.

I received my PhD from MIT AeroAstro in 2026, where I worked in the Aerospace Controls Lab (ACL) under the guidance of Professor Jonathan How. My PhD research focused on safe and fast trajectory planning in unknown dynamic environments, including spatiotemporal trajectory planning, multiagent trajectory planning robust to communication delays, heterogeneous robot teams, learning-based multiagent trajectory planning utilizing diffusion models, and perception-aware and uncertainty-aware trajectory planning. Before that, I worked on detumbling an under-actuated spacecraft with a single-axis magnetic actuator and quadcopter path planning with an onboard EMPC controller.
