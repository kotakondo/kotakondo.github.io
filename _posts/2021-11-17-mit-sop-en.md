I am publishing the essay (Statement of Purpose, SoP) required for applying to graduate schools abroad! This SoP was also used this year in the SoP writing support program of [XPLANE](https://xplane.seldoon.net/) (a Japanese nonprofit that supports students applying to graduate school abroad), so I think it brings together the basic information you need to write an SoP. Writing an SoP takes time, but once you have a reasonable draft, show it to as many people as you can and have them help you polish it! I was also edited and coached by [Daiju](https://twitter.com/DaijuUehara) (head of XPLANE / HondaJet), who really, truly helped me so much. Good luck to all of you, too!
